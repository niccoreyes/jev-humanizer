# English editing rules

Read this alongside the English pattern excerpts. These project rules take precedence over the vendored reference. Do not edit the vendored files.

## Stock email openings

Begin with the purpose of the message after the salutation. Avoid stock openers such as “I hope this message finds you well,” “I hope you are doing well,” and close variants. The local linter marks these as errors under pattern 22. A quoted example or code span is not an email opening.

## Facts and uncertainty

Keep numbers, dates, names, citations, commitments, negation, and the strength of claims. A statement that a study has a small sample, that data are missing, or that a result remains unverified is a useful limitation, not AI filler. Do not remove it merely because pattern 23 fires.
